# ZYVO: Lifestyle-Comeback

DIN-lang cover revision inspired by the client-provided win-back examples: warm ivory panel, original lifestyle photography, overlapping product circle and blue comeback seal. Text and brand mark stay separate/editable. All three recipient product variants remain supported; no discount or new performance claim introduced.

Images are originals from ZYVO (see ../cart-sources.json at the parent clients level). Lifestyle source: https://zyvo.de/cdn/shop/files/zyvolifestylesneaker.jpg?v=1751623221&width=2400 (actual 1080 × 1350, placed at 88 × 99 mm, minimum effective resolution above 300 dpi). Product photos use 54 × 54 mm placement at 1200 × 1200 px. Combined artwork: 2520 × 1188 JPEG, export at 300 dpi.

Recreate SVG layouts with `node scripts/zyvo-comeback-art.mjs`; rendered JPGs are embedded in `studio/src/zyvo-comeback-art.js` and saved backend projects. `zyvoComeback` preserves recipient records and QR targets. Cart-return URLs still open the supplied product pages.
